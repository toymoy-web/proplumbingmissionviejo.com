Plumbing Services in Mission Viejo, CA

Twenty-six services, grouped by the problem you are facing, all answered by one 24/7 call line.

All Services

Find the Right Plumbing Service for Your Problem

This page lists every service on the Mission Viejo Pro Plumbing call line, grouped by what is going wrong. If you are not sure which page fits, call and describe the symptom, because the person who answers will point you to the right job. Emergency calls such as a burst pipe, a sewage backup or no hot water come first at any hour.

Each service page explains how the problem is diagnosed, what the repair involves, and which factors set the price, with the cost quoted before work starts. Pages also cite the California Plumbing Code, which is built on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), so you know what a proper job should include.

Two pages, water damage restoration and water mitigation, show a separate dedicated phone number. That is intentional, since those are a different service category from plumbing repair.

Emergencies and Water Heaters

Hot Water and Burst Pipe Services

A heater that stops at night and a pipe that bursts in a wall are the two most urgent calls in a typical home. Start with water heater repair for a failing unit or burst pipe repair for active flooding.

Water Heater Repair

No hot water, pilot trouble, leaks at valves and fittings, and a straight answer on repair versus replace.

Water Heater Installation & Replacement

Tank, tankless and heat pump replacement with strapping, permit and venting handled.

Tankless Water Heater Service

Descaling flushes, error-code diagnosis and repairs for on-demand heaters.

Burst Pipe Repair

Emergency repair of broken supply lines, with shutoff help by phone.

Drains and Sewer

Clogs, Backups and Sewer Line Work

A single slow sink is a different job from a sewage backup. Drain cleaning clears most clogs, and a line that keeps clogging is worth a camera inspection before any larger repair is considered.

Drain Cleaning & Unclogging

Kitchen, tub and main line stoppages cleared, with a camera look when clogs return.

Hydro Jetting

High-pressure cleaning for grease, scale and roots that cable machines leave behind.

Sewer Line Repair

Spot repairs and lining for cracked, rooted or offset sewer laterals.

Sewer Line Replacement

Full lateral replacement by pipe bursting or open trench.

Sewer Camera Inspection

Video inspection of the lateral before you buy, repair or replace.

Leaks and Pipes

Hidden Leaks, Water Lines and Repiping

Water that is lost before it reaches a fixture is the costliest kind. Leak detection is the starting point for a spinning meter, and aging copper that keeps leaking may need repiping rather than another patch.

Leak Detection

Acoustic, thermal and pressure tracing of hidden plumbing leaks.

Slab Leak Detection & Repair

Pinpointing and fixing supply leaks under a concrete slab.

Water Line Repair & Replacement

The buried line from the meter to the house, repaired or replaced.

Repiping

Whole-home supply pipe replacement in PEX or copper.

Sump Pump Repair & Installation

Failed, noisy or stuck sump and ejector pumps repaired or replaced, with battery backup.

Pool Leak Detection & Repair

Pressure testing and repair of pool and spa plumbing lines.

Fixtures and Gas

Toilets, Faucets, Disposals and Bathrooms

Fixture problems are usually quick to fix. A running toilet, a dripping faucet or a jammed disposal each has its own page, and a bathroom remodel starts with shower and bathtub installation.

Toilet Repair & Installation

Running, leaking and weak-flushing toilets, plus full replacements.

Faucet & Sink Repair

Drips, leaks, low flow and fixture replacement.

Garbage Disposal Repair & Installation

Jams, leaks, dead units and new installs, with the dishwasher line reconnected.

Shower & Bathtub Installation

Tub replacements, conversions and new showers built to code.

Gas Line Installation & Repair

New appliance lines, leak repairs and pressure tests with permits.

Water Quality and Compliance

Softeners, Filters and Backflow Testing

Mission Viejo's imported water leaves scale on fixtures and heaters. A water softener addresses hardness, while a filtration system addresses taste and chlorine.

Water Softener Installation & Repair

Hard water diagnosis, softener installs and repairs, with local rules checked.

Water Filtration Installation

Whole-home carbon and under-sink reverse osmosis systems.

Backflow Testing & Prevention

Annual tests, repairs and replacements for irrigation and fire lines.

Business and Water Damage

Commercial Plumbing and Water Damage Services

Restaurants, offices and multi-tenant buildings have their own page under commercial plumbing. After a flood or a burst line, the cleanup side is handled by the water damage pages below.

Commercial Plumbing

After-hours plumbing for restaurants, offices, retail units and multi-tenant buildings.

Water Damage Restoration

Extraction, drying, cleaning and repairs after a water loss.

Water Mitigation

The first hours of a loss: stopping the spread and starting the drying.
