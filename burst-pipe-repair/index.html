Emergency Burst Pipe Repair

Emergency Burst Pipe Repair in Mission Viejo

A burst pipe is a break in a water line that releases pressurized water into the house, wall or yard. It is one of the few plumbing problems where minutes matter, because a half-inch line under normal pressure can put out a great deal of water in an hour.

Burst pipe repair has two parts. The first is stopping the flow and containing the damage. The second is replacing the failed section so the line holds pressure again, which our 24/7 line schedules as one visit when possible.

Do This in the First Five Minutes

  • Close the main water shutoff, usually at the front of the house or in the meter box at the curb.
  • Open the lowest faucet in the house to drain the remaining water from the pipes.
  • Turn off the water heater so the element or burner does not run dry.
  • If water is near outlets, switch off power to that area at the breaker panel only if it is safe to reach.
  • Move electronics and valuables, and photograph the damage before cleanup.
Causes

Why Pipes Burst in a Mild Climate

Freezing is far less common in Mission Viejo than in colder states, so most bursts here have other causes. High street pressure is one. Homes on lower slopes can see higher static pressure, and the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) calls for a pressure regulator where pressure exceeds 80 psi.

Age is another. Copper supply lines in homes from the 1960s through the 1980s develop pinholes and thin spots, and a weak spot can finally let go during a pressure surge. Impact from digging, tree roots on the service line and water hammer from fast-closing valves can also cause breaks.

If more than one leak appears in a few months, the line may be failing along its length. That is the point to look at repiping instead of patching again.
