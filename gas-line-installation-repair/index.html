If You Smell Gas, Do This First

  • Leave the house immediately with everyone inside, and leave the door open if you can.
  • Do not use light switches, phones, garage door openers or anything that can spark inside.
  • From outside, call 911 or SoCalGas at 800-427-2200.
  • Do not return until the gas utility or fire department says it is safe.
  • Call a plumber only after the utility has cleared the house.
Gas Line Service

Gas Line Installation and Repair in Mission Viejo

A gas line is the piping that carries natural gas from the meter to appliances in your home. Gas line work includes adding a line for a new range, dryer, water heater or outdoor fire pit, repairing a leaking section, replacing a corroded connector, and testing the system.

Gas work is regulated for a reason. A small leak can build up indoors, and a bad joint can fail years after installation. That is why gas line work needs a permit and an inspection in California, and why the work should be done by someone who tests every joint.

Code and Safety

What the Code Requires for Gas Line Work

California's plumbing code, built on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), covers fuel gas piping. It sets materials, sizing, support, shutoffs and testing, and it requires a pressure test before the line is put in service.

New gas piping needs a permit and an inspection through the city, and your plumber should pull the permit. Sizing also matters, because a line too small for several appliances can leave a water heater starved when the range is on.

Some homeowners also ask about an automatic seismic gas shutoff valve. These are an option for existing homes, and the plumber can explain how it works. Confirm local requirements with the city.

  4. 4

    Test and inspect

    The line is pressure tested and checked for leaks, then inspected before use.
