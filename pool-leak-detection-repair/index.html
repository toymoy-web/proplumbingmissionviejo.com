Pool Leaks

Pool and Spa Leak Detection in Mission Viejo

Pool leak detection is the process of finding where a pool or spa is losing water, using dye, pressure tests and listening equipment. Water loss can come from the plumbing lines, the equipment pad, the skimmer, a light niche or the shell itself, and each has a different fix.

Pools and spas in Mission Viejo include private backyard pools and the shared pools at gated associations. Casta del Sol has two recreation centers with pools and spas, and Palmia is built around Club Palmia and its resort pool, so association and HOA managers also call about plumbing losses.

The Bucket Test: Leak or Evaporation

  • Fill a bucket with pool water and set it on a pool step so its water level matches the pool's.
  • Mark the water level inside the bucket and the pool level outside it, then turn off the pump and any automatic fill.
  • Check again after 24 hours. Both levels should drop by about the same amount if evaporation is the cause.
  • If the pool drops more than the bucket, you likely have a leak. Repeat with the pump running to see whether the plumbing is involved.

Honest Limits

What We Handle and What a Pool Builder Handles

We find and repair leaks in the plumbing lines that run to and from your pool, including supply, return and equipment connections. We do not resurface plaster, replace tile, or repair a cracked shell, which are pool contractor jobs. If the leak is in the shell, we will tell you and point you to the right trade.

Pool plumbing in California is covered by the California Plumbing Code, built on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), along with pool and spa safety requirements for drains. A line replacement may need a permit.

If you manage an association pool, record the water meter reading and the daily level before the visit. That log helps the plumber separate a plumbing loss from evaporation and heavy use.
