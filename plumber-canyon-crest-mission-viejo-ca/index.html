The Neighborhood

Plumbing Help in Canyon Crest

Canyon Crest is a large residential community in Mission Viejo, California, near Lake Mission Viejo. If you live here, you can call (949) 325-3122 and give your street for leaks, drains, water heaters or outdoor plumbing.

Local guides describe Canyon Crest as a community of larger homes with sizable backyards, and that points to a particular kind of plumbing call. More outdoor space usually means more irrigation, more exterior lines and more things connected to the water supply than a compact lot has.

Where It Is

Where Canyon Crest Sits in Mission Viejo

The Lake Mission Viejo Association lists Canyon Crest as District 56, with 979 homes, which makes it one of the largest districts on the list. Two local guides place it near the lake and describe it as a gated community, while another local guide says gates in the area are not confirmed and a gate does not establish any particular security service. We therefore ask you to tell dispatch how to enter.

Membership in the lake association gives access to the lake, and that is separate from your home's plumbing. The association assessment does not cover pipes, drains or water heaters inside or outside your house. Public listings for Canyon Crest Drive show ZIP 92692.

Plumbing Notes

What Larger Lots Mean for Plumbing

This section is general plumbing information, not a finding about any one Canyon Crest home.

Outdoor water use is the first thing to look at on a larger lot. Irrigation valves, hose bibs, a pool or spa and an outdoor kitchen all connect to the supply. A leak outside the house may not show up as a puddle, but a meter that moves with every fixture off can reveal it, and the bill often rises first.

Backflow devices protect the drinking water supply from irrigation and pool connections, and they usually need periodic testing. Check your water district or city for the rule that applies to your address.

Gas lines are another outdoor topic. A new barbecue, fire feature or pool heater needs a branch line sized for the load and a pressure test. This is work that should not be done informally, and it is covered by permit rules in California.

A pool can lose water in two ways, evaporation and a leak. A simple bucket test separates them, and a pool leak is a different job from a house leak.

Before You Call From Canyon Crest

  • Give dispatch your street and tell them how to enter the community.
  • Check your water meter with every fixture and irrigation zone off.
  • Know which outdoor lines and valves you have, such as irrigation, pool and gas.
  • Find your backflow device and note any test tag.
  • Find the water district name on your bill.
