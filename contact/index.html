Hours

The call line is staffed around the clock. There is no walk-in counter, and service is by dispatch.

What to Tell Dispatch When You Call

  • Your street address, ZIP code and the nearest cross street.
  • What is happening: where the water is, what you see, hear or smell, and when it started.
  • Whether the main water shutoff is already closed.
  • A callback number and any gate, HOA or building access instructions.
  • Whether this is an emergency or a planned job, since planned jobs get a free estimate.
After You Call

What Happens Next

Your call is connected to an independent local plumbing provider who serves your area. They give you the price before emergency work starts and an estimated arrival window based on traffic and call volume.

Permits, where the job needs one, follow the California Plumbing Code, built on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). If you have a gas smell, leave the house and call 911 or SoCalGas at 800-427-2200 before you call us. If sewage or water is spreading, close the main shutoff and tell dispatch.

Email is for non-urgent questions, such as a page correction or a general question about a service. Email is not monitored for emergencies.
