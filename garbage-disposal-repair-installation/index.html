Disposals

Garbage Disposal Repair and Installation in Mission Viejo

A garbage disposal grinds food scraps into particles small enough to pass through the drain. Repair covers jams, motor failures, leaks and electrical faults. Installation covers replacing an old unit or adding one under a sink that never had it.

Most disposals last about 8 to 15 years. A unit that hums but does not spin is jammed, one that does nothing may have a tripped reset or a dead motor, and one that drips has failed seals or a loose connection.

Safe Steps Before You Call

  • Switch the disposal off at the wall switch, and unplug it if there is a cord.
  • Look under the sink for the red reset button on the bottom of the unit and press it once.
  • Never put your hand into the grinding chamber. Use tongs for visible items.
  • Skip chemical drain cleaners in a disposal that is backing up.
Dishwasher Connection

The Dishwasher Detail That Trips Up New Installs

Most dishwashers drain through the disposal. A new disposal has a knockout plug inside the dishwasher inlet, and if it is not removed, the dishwasher cannot drain. This is one of the most common mistakes in a do-it-yourself swap.

A plumber also connects the dishwasher line with a loop or air gap to keep wastewater from flowing back, as the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) and the California Plumbing Code call for. The line also needs to be reattached after a swap.

The Process

How a Disposal Install Goes

  1. 1

    Remove

    Power is shut off and the old unit is disconnected from the flange and drain.

  2. 2

    Mount

    The new unit is secured to the sink flange and aligned with the trap.

  3. 3

    Connect

    The drain, dishwasher line and electrical connection are made, with the knockout plug removed.

  4. 4

    Test

    The unit runs with water and the connections are checked for leaks.

Local Notes

Disposals and Mission Viejo Sewer Lines

Food scraps and grease that pass through a disposal travel into older drain and sewer lines. In homes from the 1960s through the 1980s, grease sticking to the pipe wall is a common cause of kitchen backups. Keep fats out of the unit and run cold water while it grinds.

If the sink backs up even though the unit works, the problem is in the drain, and drain cleaning is the right call. Leaks around the sink flange or trap overlap with faucet and sink repair. We install disposals in nearby Ladera Ranch as well.
