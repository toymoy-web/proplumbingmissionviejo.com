Service Lines

Water Line Repair and Replacement in Mission Viejo

The water service line is the buried pipe that carries water from the district meter to your house. It is separate from the plumbing inside the home, which is covered under repiping. A leak in the service line wastes water before it ever reaches a fixture, and it often goes unseen because the pipe is underground.

The meter is the dividing line. The district owns the main and the meter, and in general the homeowner owns and maintains the pipe from the meter to the house. Santa Margarita Water District, Moulton Niguel Water District and El Toro Water District each set their own rules, so confirm yours.

Emergency Service

Emergency Water Line Leaks and No Water at the House

Water bubbling up in a yard, a soaked patch near the meter box or a house with no water at all calls for an emergency visit. Close the house shutoff and check whether your neighbors have water, since a district problem is not a line on your side of the meter.

If the meter runs with the house shutoff closed, the leak is on the buried line. Call the 24/7 line, describe where the ground is wet and we will locate the break before anyone digs.

Symptoms

Signs of a Leaking Service Line

Meter turns with fixtures off

The most reliable sign. Shut off everything inside and watch the dial or low-flow indicator.

A wet or soft patch outside

Water rising to the surface along the line, often between the meter and the foundation.

Falling water pressure

A growing leak steals pressure before the water reaches the house.

A higher district bill

The meter counts the water that escapes underground.

Confirm It Before You Dig

  • Turn off every fixture and appliance, then check whether the meter still moves.
  • Close the main shutoff at the house and see whether the meter stops. If it keeps moving, the leak is between the meter and the shutoff.
  • Do not dig on your own. Call 811 first so utility lines are marked.
  • Tell your district if you find a leak, since some offer bill adjustments after documented repairs.

Local Rules

Pressure, Permits and Utilities in Mission Viejo

The Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), which California's plumbing code is built on, calls for a pressure regulator where supply pressure exceeds 80 psi. Many Mission Viejo homes sit on slopes where street pressure can run high, and a failed or missing regulator is a common cause of service line stress.

A new service line normally needs a permit, and work in the public right-of-way follows your district's connection rules. A plumber should handle the permit and call 811 so gas, electric and telecom lines are marked before anyone digs.

Mission Viejo also has a recycled water system that irrigates parks and medians. Potable and recycled lines must stay separate, which is one reason to mark and locate before excavating.

What Water Line Work Costs

Related Services

Service Line, Slab or Inside the House

If the leak is under a concrete slab inside the house, see slab leak detection. If you only know the meter moves, leak detection separates a service line leak from an interior one before anyone digs.

A sewer lateral is a different pipe and sometimes runs near the water line. When both are failing, sewer line replacement can be coordinated with the water line to share trenching. We also service nearby San Juan Capistrano properties.

Caller Questions

The Line From the Meter to the House

Most water line calls start the same way. The meter keeps running after the house shutoff is closed, or a wet patch shows up between the meter and the house. We confirm with a short test, locate the break and give a price for a spot repair, a new section or a full replacement.

If you know roughly how long the line is, say so. A short run from the street is a different job than a long run across a lot.

Emergency Triage

No Water or Weak Pressure at the House

A house with no water, or a sudden pressure drop at every tap, needs a quick check of the main shutoff, the pressure regulator and the line. If neighbors also lost water, the district is the first call.

If the problem is on your side of the meter, tell dispatch when the water changed and whether the bill is current with your provider. We will plan the visit around the likeliest cause.

Also Common

Yard Leaks and Meter Box Leaks

A leak at the meter box fittings or a wet yard next to the box is common and often fixed quickly. If the wet spot is on the district's side of the meter, your water provider handles it, and we will tell you which side it is.

Local Coverage

Where We Work Across Mission Viejo

The buried line from the meter to the house is the same age as the tract it sits in. Homes built from 1966 near Montanoso Recreation Center and the early Marguerite Parkway streets have the oldest service lines, while Painted Trails lines are much newer.
