Where the 24/7 Plumbing Line Reaches

The Mission Viejo Pro Plumbing line is based in Mission Viejo and covers the city and the south Orange County communities that touch it. If your address is inside one of the areas below, call (949) 325-3122 any hour. If you are near the edge of a ZIP code, call and dispatch will confirm coverage by street.

Mission Viejo itself is covered in ZIP codes 92691 and 92692. The city was planned from 1963, the first families moved in during 1966, and it incorporated on March 31, 1988. Casta del Sol, a gated 55+ community, has 1,927 homes built from 1972 to 1987, and Palmia has 901 built from 1988 to 1999. ZIP Codes Within About 10 Miles

Reading Your Bill

How to Tell Which Water Agency Serves You

Your water bill is the quickest way to learn which agency serves your home. The agency name and account number sit at the top, and the same bill shows your meter reading and usage. That agency is the one to call about a leak in the street, a pressure complaint or a meter that looks wrong.

The plumbing on your side of the meter is your responsibility, which is where the 24/7 line comes in. A leak that shows up between the meter and the house, a clog in your sewer lateral or a failed water heater are jobs for a plumber, not the district. Knowing the boundary saves a phone call to the wrong place at the wrong hour.

Water agencies in this area also run recycled water systems for irrigation. Properties that use recycled water have backflow devices that need annual testing, which our backflow testing page covers.
